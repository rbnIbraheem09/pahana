## Design Context

### Users
- **Health workers** (estate medical assistants, public health midwives) doing rounds in Sri Lankan estate/rural areas. They work on a laptop or tablet, often with no signal, sometimes in bright daylight, and with patients waiting. They need to enter readings fast and trust that nothing is lost.
- **Doctors** at a district clinic facing a queue far larger than they can see in a day. They need to know who is urgent in seconds, and why, and to act in one keystroke.
- **Patients** never see the app. They receive a short SMS in Sinhala, Tamil or English with no diagnosis and no numbers.

### Brand Personality
Three words: **steady, exact, humane.** Pahana (පහන) is the clay oil lamp: warm light in a dark room. The interface should feel like a calm, capable colleague. Urgency is carried by colour and order, never by alarmist decoration. Emotional goal: *confidence* for the worker ("it's saved, it's safe"), *clarity* for the doctor ("I know who's next and why").

### Aesthetic Direction
- Dark navy base (Yashod's reference), with a "Daylight" light theme for outdoor field use.
- The triage colours are the only loud colours: red, amber, green. They always come with a shape (▲ red, ◆ amber, ● green) and a text label, so colour-blind users are never relying on colour alone.
- One accent blue for primary actions. Neutrals are tinted toward the navy hue (OKLCH, hue ≈ 258).
- Type: Bricolage Grotesque (display, big numbers), Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (UI, chosen for reading accuracy), Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono (IDs), Noto Sans Sinhala/Tamil.
- Anti-references: generic SaaS dashboards, glassmorphism, neon-on-dark "AI" palettes, gradient text, side-stripe card borders, hero-metric templates.

### Design Principles
1. **Order is the interface.** The most important thing is always at the top, left and largest. Sorting does the work, so decoration doesn't have to.
2. **Every flag explains itself.** No colour without a reason in plain words.
3. **Motion means something happened.** Animate state changes: a record saved, sent or synced, a patient moving through the queue. Never animate for its own sake. Exponential ease-out, no bounce, and respect reduced motion.
4. **Fast hands.** Keyboard-first, auto-advance fields, optimistic UI, no confirmation for reversible actions.
5. **Native, not webby.** The desktop app behaves like a Mac/Windows app: seamless chrome, default cursors, no stray selection, real menus hidden where the OS puts them.
